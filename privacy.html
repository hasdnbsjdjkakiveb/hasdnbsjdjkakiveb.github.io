Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2025

At Webelo Designs, your privacy is important to us. This policy outlines what information we collect, how it's used, and your rights — with full transparency.

What Information Do We Collect?

We do not collect any personal data through this website.

The only information we may have access to is what you voluntarily provide, such as when you:

  • Email us directly
  • Call or text us
  • Share information during a service inquiry or consultation

Data Storage

We do not store any visitor or user data through this website. We do not maintain any user database, and no form data is collected or saved.

Third-Party Services

While we do not use any analytics or ad tracking tools, this site may include minimal third-party assets such as Google Fonts or icon libraries (e.g., Font Awesome). These services may make external requests to load fonts or icons but do not collect any identifiable personal data from you through our site.

Your Privacy Rights

If you have contacted us and wish to have any of your messages or information removed from our records, simply email us and we’ll take care of it immediately.
